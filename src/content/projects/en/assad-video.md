## What it is

The ASSaD-Video surveillance system. I worked mostly on the **frontend** side.

## Stack

- JavaScript: **jQuery**, **jQuery-ui**, **AngularJS**, **fabric.js** (interactive
  canvas work — floor plans, zones, markup).
- Markup with **HTML/CSS**, **Bootstrap**.
- Some server-side **Java**.

One of my early large projects — where I built up experience in markup, canvas
work, and building interfaces before the era of modern frameworks.
