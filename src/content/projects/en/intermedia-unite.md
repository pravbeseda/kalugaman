## What it is

**Unite** is a business messenger that bundles chats, telephony, SMS chats, video
calls and file storage into one product. **Contact Center** is a tool for customer
service teams: agents handle requests from voice, email and text queues, with
statistics collected along the way. Contact Center runs standalone or embedded
inside Unite.

## Platforms

A web version on **Angular** (plus some legacy React) and a desktop app on
**Electron**. Since 2023, a Unite Teams Integration plugin lets people use the app
directly inside Microsoft Teams.
