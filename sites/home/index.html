HHome

Home

A new-tab page for Chrome.

Home replaces the new tab with the bookmarks of a folder you choose, laid out as a visual board, and widgets you can drag between three columns: recently closed tabs, most visited sites, your open GitHub pull requests, free disk space, AI agent usage limits and the status of your own monitoring hub.

Everything it shows comes from your browser, your computer and the services you connect yourself. The extension has no account, no server and no analytics.
